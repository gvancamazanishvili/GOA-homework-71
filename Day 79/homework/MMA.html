What is MMA

MMA is a full-contact combat sport that combines techniques from various martial arts disciplines including boxing, wrestling, Brazilian jiu-jitsu, Muay Thai, judo, and karate. Fighters can use striking (punches, kicks, elbows, knees) and grappling (takedowns, submissions, ground control) to defeat their opponent.
